FAQ

What's the dress code?

Our wedding will be formal attire, but please keep in mind that the event will take place outdoors. We suggest wearing shoes and fabrics that are comfortable for grass and outdoor conditions, while still keeping it classy.

Will there be an open bar or cash bar?

We'll be having a Toonie Bar (cash only) for alcoholic drinks, so please bring a few coins or small bills with you. Non-alcoholic beverages will be available throughout the evening.

Are the ceremony and reception at the same location?

Yes! Both the ceremony and the reception will take place at the beautiful Riverview Orchard in Keswick, NB. No need to travel between venues, just relax and enjoy the whole celebration in one spot.
